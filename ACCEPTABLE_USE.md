# Acceptable Use Policy

Search Ninja Lite is provided for ethical public-source research, business intelligence, document discovery, and source verification.

## Allowed Uses

You may use Search Ninja Lite for:

- Business and company research
- Competitor and market research
- Public document discovery
- Government and regulatory research
- Academic and technical research
- News, press, podcast, webinar, and conference research
- Professional public-profile context when tied to a legitimate business, journalistic, academic, or public-interest purpose
- Source verification and fact checking
- Training teams to perform safer public-source research

## Prohibited Uses

Do not use Search Ninja Lite for:

- Credential harvesting or exposed secret discovery
- Leak-site, breach, or paste-site searching
- Searching for passwords, tokens, API keys, private keys, configs, backups, databases, logs, dumps, or authentication material
- Finding admin panels, control panels, login portals, hidden endpoints, cameras, surveillance systems, IoT interfaces, DVRs, NVRs, or internet-exposed devices
- Collecting personal addresses, phone numbers, voter records, dating profiles, family records, private accounts, or people-search aggregator data
- Doxxing, stalking, harassment, intimidation, or invasive profiling
- Identifying, aggregating, or exposing private personal information
- Unauthorized access, exploitation, evasion, or bypassing authentication or paywalls
- Malware delivery, phishing, social engineering, or abuse of any system or person
- Violating applicable laws, contracts, platform terms, or professional rules

## Research Boundaries

Publicly indexed does not always mean appropriate to collect, summarize, or republish. Use judgment. Minimize unnecessary personal data. Prefer business, professional, organizational, government, academic, or source-verification use cases.

When in doubt, choose the least invasive method that answers the legitimate research question.

## Disclaimer

This repository is provided for lawful, ethical research. You are responsible for how you use it and for complying with applicable laws and platform terms.
