# Safe Example Requests

These examples show how Search Ninja Lite should translate plain-language requests into safe research patterns.

## Company Research

Request:

```text
Research Acme Corp and find official sources, recent news, public documents, and competitor context.
```

Useful query patterns:

```text
"Acme Corp" site:acme.example
"Acme Corp" "press release" OR news OR announcement
"Acme Corp" "annual report" filetype:pdf
"Acme Corp" competitors OR alternatives
```

## Competitor Research

Request:

```text
Find competitors for a local AI consulting business serving small and mid-sized companies.
```

Useful query patterns:

```text
"AI consulting" "small business" "Michigan"
"AI automation" "small business" "consulting"
"AI consulting" "case study" "small business"
"AI consulting" "pricing" "small business"
```

## Public Document Discovery

Request:

```text
Find PDF reports and guidance about cybersecurity requirements for small businesses.
```

Useful query patterns:

```text
"small business" cybersecurity requirements filetype:pdf
"small business" cybersecurity guidance site:gov filetype:pdf
"small business" cybersecurity site:nist.gov filetype:pdf
"small business" cybersecurity checklist filetype:pdf
```

## Source Verification

Request:

```text
Verify whether a vendor's claim about AI adoption among small businesses is supported by reliable sources.
```

Useful query patterns:

```text
"exact claim phrase"
"AI adoption" "small business" report filetype:pdf
"AI adoption" "small business" site:gov OR site:edu
"AI adoption" "small business" survey
```

## Professional Public Context

Request:

```text
Find public professional context for Jane Smith related to her cybersecurity speaking and writing.
```

Useful query patterns:

```text
"Jane Smith" cybersecurity speaker OR panelist OR presenter
"Jane Smith" cybersecurity interview OR podcast OR webinar
"Jane Smith" cybersecurity article OR publication
"Jane Smith" site:linkedin.com/in cybersecurity
```

Boundary: keep the research focused on public professional work. Do not collect private addresses, personal contact details, family information, dating profiles, or unrelated personal material.
