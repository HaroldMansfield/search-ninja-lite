# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they keep Search Ninja Lite safe, useful, and focused on ethical public-source research.

## Good Contributions

Good additions include:

- Safer query templates for business research
- Public document discovery patterns
- Government and regulatory source patterns
- Academic and technical research patterns
- Source verification workflows
- Clearer safety language
- Documentation fixes

## Contributions That Will Not Be Accepted

Do not submit templates or examples for:

- Credential discovery
- Leak-site or breach searching
- Paste-site searching
- Exposed secrets
- Admin panels or login portals
- Surveillance systems or internet-exposed devices
- Private personal data aggregation
- Doxxing, stalking, harassment, or invasive profiling
- Unauthorized access, exploitation, or evasion

## Pull Request Checklist

Before submitting a pull request, confirm:

- The change supports ethical public-source research.
- The change avoids high-risk operator categories.
- The examples are business, academic, government, technical, or source-verification oriented.
- No secrets, credentials, private data, or sensitive targets are included.
- Markdown is clear and free of em dashes.
